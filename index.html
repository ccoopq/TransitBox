Data details

Shared tracking, separate identity and payment outputs.

Boarding → Onboard gallery → Alighting search → Remove match

Video and tracking
Video and result files are supplied locally. Saved GHR-VLM tracks, activity labels, clip boundaries and payment outputs are synchronized to video time.

Passenger identity
TransReID ViT-Base + JPM extracts appearance features from the shared passenger crops. Boarding creates a gallery entry. Alighting selects the most similar earlier onboard boarding entry and removes it immediately. Stop-local Track IDs never substitute for an alighting appearance search.

ReID run
No local data loaded.

Matching policy
Direct nearest-neighbor cosine matching, with no default similarity cutoff, candidate competition or provisional decisions. A nearest match is an algorithmic assignment, not verified identity accuracy. An empty eligible gallery leaves an exit unmatched. Repeated clips of one behavior do not add or remove passengers twice.

Matching scope
Each video has its own gallery. The two videos are not assumed to share a clock or represent the same journey.

Payment and counts
Exit records link to the matched passenger’s boarding payment. “At boarding” identifies inherited payment data. Payment charts count original classified clips, without counting inherited values again. The onboard figure counts gallery entries at playback time, not verified occupancy.

Videos and passenger records are not included in the public site.
